Company Introduction

Insight System is a digital competency management and development platform dedicated to the oil and gas industry. We deeply integrate industry training needs with digital technology to provide enterprises with end-to-end solutions from training planning, course learning, skill assessment to certification issuance.

Since our establishment in 2020, we have served more than 50 energy companies and cumulatively trained and certified over 12,000 professionals. The system covers core disciplines including HSE safety management, well engineering, mechanical engineering, and electrical engineering, supporting multi-level skill certification and a three-tier verification process.

Our mission is to drive the standardization and digital transformation of talent development in the energy industry through technological innovation, helping enterprises build efficient and reliable competency management systems.
