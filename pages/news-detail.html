2026 HSE Safety Training Plan Released

HSE Safety Training

To further enhance the safety awareness and emergency response capabilities of all personnel, and to ensure safe and stable oil and gas production operations, the Company Safety Training Department has officially released the 2026 Annual HSE Safety Training Plan. Centered on the core philosophy of “prevention first, full participation, continuous improvement,” this year’s training plan systematically outlines six major training modules covering all employees and contractor personnel.

I. Overall Training Objectives

The 2026 HSE Safety Training aims to achieve the following core objectives:

  • Achieve 100% HSE training coverage for all employees, with 100% certification rate for key positions
  • Zero major safety incidents, with a year-over-year reduction of 15% in general incident rates
  • Employee safety knowledge assessment pass rate no lower than 95%
  • Emergency response drill participation rate no lower than 90%

II. Six Training Modules

1. Well Control Safety Training

Targeting drilling, completion, and workover operations, this module provides specialized training on well control equipment operation, blowout prevention, and emergency response. Trainees include drilling engineers, rig operators, and field supervisors. The program spans 40 class hours and combines theoretical instruction with simulation exercises.

2. Offshore Operations Safety

Designed for offshore platform personnel, this module focuses on marine survival, firefighting, helicopter underwater escape training (HUET), and confined space entry safety. All offshore personnel must complete annual refresher training and pass the assessment before boarding platforms.

3. Emergency Response & Rescue

Regional emergency response teams will conduct comprehensive drills covering scenarios such as fire and explosion, oil and gas leaks, personnel injury, and natural disasters. The Tianjin, Zhanjiang, and Shanghai bases will host regional emergency drill competitions in June, August, and October respectively.

4. Hazardous Work Permit Management

This module strengthens the execution of Permit to Work (PTW) system training, covering the approval process, safety measures, and supervision requirements for high-risk operations including hot work, confined space entry, working at heights, and lifting operations. Training will incorporate hands-on online exercises using the Insight System PTW module.

5. Occupational Health & Protection

Occupational health knowledge promotion covering noise protection, chemical exposure control, ergonomics, and mental health. Comprehensive occupational health check-ups will be organized for all employees, with individual health records established.

6. Environmental Protection & Compliance

Aligned with national environmental regulations and industry standards, training covers waste management, oil spill prevention and control, carbon emission management, and ecological restoration. In support of the company’s green and low-carbon development strategy, new specialized training modules on carbon footprint accounting and emission reduction technologies have been added.

III. Training Schedule

This year’s training program will commence in May 2026 and conclude in December 2026, progressing through three phases:

  1. Phase 1 (May–Jun): Basic safety knowledge training and assessment, covering all employees
  2. Phase 2 (Jul–Sep): Specialized skills training and emergency drills, focusing on key positions
  3. Phase 3 (Oct–Dec): Comprehensive capability evaluation and best practice sharing
Safety is the lifeline of enterprise development. Every employee is the primary person responsible for workplace safety. We encourage everyone to actively participate in training, internalize safety principles, and put them into practice — together building a strong safety culture for the company.

IV. Registration & Assessment

Regional units should complete participant registration through the Insight System’s Training Management module before April 30. A unified assessment will be conducted after training, with results incorporated into individual annual performance evaluations and Skill Profiles. Outstanding performers will receive the “HSE Safety Champion” certificate of honor and corresponding rewards.
